## What is under review

The two statistical rows, each for the per-sample limit, the target yield and
the confidence level:

- **Input-referred offset, random component**: Option A (random-only limit),
  B (total-offset limit) or C (aggregate-only), and the DR-0002 residual-(b)
  statistical-basis decision that must precede final ratification.
- **CMRR, mismatch-inclusive**: Option A (the 26.68 dB aggregate as a
  per-sample limit), B (a product-justified dB limit) or C (aggregate-only).
- **Claim form**: the per-grid-point exact (Clopper-Pearson) binomial claim,
  the required-N table, the inconclusive-outcome rule, and the per-point versus
  joint confidence choice.

The record proposes no numeric limit; the reviewers decide per its "Decision
requested of the ratifiers" list.

## Process

1. EE key and market key each post a verdict on this PR with
   `ratify-key.sh apply`, as a holder that is neither this PR's author nor the
   design's author, distinct per key.
2. The PR then waits for the operator, who ratifies. Agents do not ratify and
   do not run `release`.
