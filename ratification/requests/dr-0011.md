## What is under review

The exact rows are the "What is submitted for review" table of
DR-0011: GBW, phase margin, slew rate, PSRR+ (>= 26.9 dB), quiescent power and
supply / temperature, at the 45-point `mos_tt/ss/ff/sf/fs` x `{-40, 27, 125} C`
x `{1.08, 1.2, 1.32} V` grid. Revision 2 (after the operator send-back on head `44d9e7ec`) removes
input-referred noise, systematic offset and output-swing tracking span from the
submitted bounds: they appear only as measured values with status (108.949
uVrms, 21.9273527 mV, 0.570957773 V; FAIL against their unratified working
limits) in "Rows not submitted as bounds". Revision 3 restates PSRR+ to >= 26.9 dB (operator ruling r5-q75
option 3), citing the current
record `sim/cmrr-psrr/klt/records/20261015-103521-0f41068`, and "What the
block is for at these numbers" answers the market key's escalation. Revision 4 (operator send-back r5-q89 option 2) moves open-loop
gain, mismatch-inclusive CMRR and swing headroom out of the submitted rows into
"Rows not submitted as bounds", and states in the binding Status clause that
the 1.87 dB PSRR+ floor in `spec/target-spec.md`, `spec/integrator.json`,
`selection.json` and the minted report is superseded pending gap 6.
Revision 5 records swing as the common output range across measured corners
at each supply. The old-topology swing evidence can land with #333. Swing,
CMRR and gain remain in “Rows not submitted as bounds.” The carried market
figures for CMRR and gain are unverified. [Revision 5 report](../../signoff/characterization/reports/20261122-215711-d825300.md)

## Process

1. Ruling r5-q96 selected a replacement topology. Under amendment r5-q99,
   remeasure every DR-0011 row on that design, including swing and the
   45-point × 300-trial CMRR grid. [Issue #332](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-opamp/issues/332)
2. After remeasurement, the EE and market keys each post a verdict on the
   revised head of #303 with `ratify-key.sh apply`. The two holders must
   be distinct and independent of the PR and design authors.
3. Once both keys approve, the operator ratifies DR-0011 on the new topology.
   Agents do not ratify and do not run `release`.
