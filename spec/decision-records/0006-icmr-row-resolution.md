## Context

The ICMR row (`spec/target-spec.md`, "Input common-mode range") is measured
but unratified, and it records a finding no issue resolves: at 15 of 45 PVT
points the measured lower bound lies above the design's own nominal
`Vcm = VDD/2`, so a part biased at mid-rail has a tail device `M5` below its
saturation knee there. The row names three resolutions: a higher-than-mid-rail
input common mode, a re-biased tail, or explicit acceptance of a knee-region
tail. This record chooses among them using committed evidence only.

## Evidence (committed; reproducible offline)

Source: `sim/input-cmr/records/20260921-174405-65f5fb4.csv`, regenerated by
`python3 sim/input-cmr/check_icmr_evidence.py` (stdlib only, no simulator).
These are nominal matched devices, deterministic corner-worst-case, `CL = 2 pF`
[DR-1], differential input pinned at zero, `op_pass=1` at all points. Values
are as printed in the CSV (9 decimals); no precision beyond the CSV is claimed.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Points / unique PVT IDs | 45 / 45 |
| Points with `contains_vcm = 0` (mid-rail outside input-stage range) | **15** |
| Max over points of `icmr_lo_v - vcm_v` | 0.100129553 V at `mos_ss_-40C_1.08V` |
| Lower bound, binding (max of `icmr_lo_v`) | 0.640129553 V at `mos_ss_-40C_1.08V` (`m5_headroom`) |
| Upper bound, binding (min of `icmr_hi_v`) | 0.795393113 V at `mos_fs_125C_1.08V` (`pair_saturation`) |
| **Input-stage all-grid intersection** | **[0.640129553, 0.795393113] V** (non-empty; width 0.155 V) |
| Worst-span corner interval (a different quantity) | [0.615799253, 0.869005017] V, span 0.253205764 V at `mos_ss_125C_1.08V` |
| Closed-loop-buffer `buffer_use_*` all-grid intersection | [0.640129553, 0.772224158] V (upper bound 23 mV lower than input-stage) |
| Worst `buffer_use_span_v` | 0.161187096 V at `mos_ss_125C_1.08V` (interval [0.615799253, 0.776986349]) |

The 15 points: 1.08 V at `mos_tt` x 3 temperatures, `mos_ss` x 3, `mos_sf` x 3
and `mos_fs` at -40 / 27 C; 1.20 V at `mos_ss` x 3 and `mos_sf` at -40 C. The
checker counts them from the `contains_vcm` column, which is authoritative.
Lower mechanism is `m5_headroom` and upper is `pair_saturation` at all 45
points.

Distinctions that must not be conflated:

1. **Input-stage ICMR** (the table's intersection) is the range in which M5
   and the pair stay saturated. It is not a usability claim for a closed-loop
   buffer, whose `buffer_use_*` interval also intersects `sim/output-swing/`
   reach and is narrower (upper bound 0.7722 V, worst span 0.1612 V).
2. A single worst-span interval is a per-corner quantity; a window valid at
   every PVT point is the intersection. Only the intersection is a guarantee
   across the grid.
3. **Rounding.** Displayed "0.6401-0.7954 V" rounds outward (lower down, upper
   up) and is not a guarantee: the checker shows `[0.6401, 0.7954]` is
   uncovered at both binding points. Outward-safe (lower up, upper down) at 1
   mV is **[0.641, 0.795] V** (input stage), **[0.641, 0.772] V** (buffer);
   both are verified to be covered at all 45 points by the checker/tests.
4. The window is absolute volts, over all three rails. Mid-rail is 0.54 V at
   1.08 V, 0.60 V at 1.20 V and 0.66 V at 1.32 V, so mid-rail sits below 0.641
   V at the two lower rails (and at 1.32 V all points contain mid-rail).

Limitations: nominal matched devices only (mismatch shifts the real ICMR; no
statistics are claimed); pre-layout; LV devices at 1.2 V +/- 10 %; DC
operating-point criterion (saturation), not a gain/CMRR-degradation criterion;
the lower-bound `Vdsat(M5)` uses a same-bias replica probe and the upper bound
a constant-current `Vth` convention (see the bench README).

## Consumer requirements (`spec/consumers.md`)

- **Bandgap (`sg13g2-bandgap`)**: needs a fixed ~0.7-0.8 V common mode, but at
  a **3.3 V** rail. This block is LV-only at 1.2 V; the measurement does **not**
  establish suitability at the consumer's rail. Unknown: whether a 1.2 V
  derived rail or an HV variant is intended.
- **LDO (`sg13g2-ldo`)**: the error amp is an ideal placeholder; the
  common-mode requirement is **unstated**. Unknown, with a question for key
  review: what feedback/reference common mode (as a fraction of which rail) does
  the regulator present to the error amp?
- No consumer states a requirement that selects between the options. The
  window is therefore recorded as a property of this block, not as meeting any
  consumer's need.

## Options

1. **Higher-than-mid-rail input common mode (recommended).** Declare the
   usable `Vcm` window explicitly (above) and require integrators to bias
   inside it. No sizing change; reversible; consistent with the bandgap's
   stated 0.7-0.8 V need as context only. Cost: the part is not usable with
   inputs at `VDD/2` at the low rails, and a fixed absolute `Vcm` must be
   chosen by the integrator inside a window about 0.155 V wide at 1.08-1.32 V.
2. **Re-bias the tail** (e.g. lower `Vdsat(M5)`). Moves the lower bound but is a
   sizing change; all benches must be re-run and ratified bounds (DR-2) are at
   risk. Not authorized here.
3. **Accept knee-region operation at mid-rail.** Rejected for now: it accepts
   an operating risk (tail starvation, the slew collapse the slew-rate study
   saw under common-mode excursion) that this draft has no authority to accept.

## Recommendation

Adopt option 1 as a **proposal for later ratification**: one bound per ICMR row,
guaranteed (outward-safe) over all 45 points and stated for this block's LV
1.2 V +/- 10 % envelope:

- Input-stage ICMR: **0.641 V <= Vcm <= 0.795 V** (binding corners as above).
- Closed-loop buffer usable interval, if the keys want the buffer row:
  **0.641 V <= Vcm <= 0.772 V** (formula: intersection of `buffer_use_lo_v` /
  `buffer_use_hi_v` over all points, rounded outward-safe).
- The nominal `Vcm = VDD/2` is **not** a supported bias point at 1.08 V.

Open caveat for the keys: ratified rows measured at `Vcm = VDD/2` (e.g. slew
rate, DR-2) were taken at points where mid-rail is outside the window; whether
those results transfer to a `Vcm` inside the window is **unknown** and is not
tested by this draft.
