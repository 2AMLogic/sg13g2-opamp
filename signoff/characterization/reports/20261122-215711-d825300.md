# DR-0011 revision 5 — CMRR, gain and output swing on the DR-0001 topology

This report supersedes the [November 4 re-derivation](20261104-190000-1907585.md)
for issue #332. It uses the same committed CSVs to characterize the original
DR-0001 sizing. The measurements use a 2 pF load with no resistive load.

## Output range

The swing bench defines the endpoints by a −6 dB incremental-gain tracking
criterion. At each supply, the range common to all 15 process/temperature
points starts at the highest `vout_min_track_v` and ends at the lowest
`vout_max_track_v`. To keep the displayed interval inside every measured
interval, the table rounds the lower endpoint up and the upper endpoint down
to four decimal places. Each span is the difference of those displayed
endpoints. The EE key owns the range definition. [Swing CSV](../../../sim/output-swing/records/20260921-151759-707b34c.csv)

| VDD | Guaranteed Vout range on the measured grid | Span |
|---|---|---|
| 1.08 V | 0.2069 V to 0.7722 V | 0.5653 V |
| 1.20 V | 0.2151 V to 0.8893 V | 0.6742 V |
| 1.32 V | 0.2231 V to 1.0009 V | 0.7778 V |

At 1.08 V the 0.2069 V lower endpoint comes from 0.206837796 V at
`mos_sf_125C_1.08V`. The 0.7722 V upper endpoint comes from 0.772224158 V at
`mos_fs_125C_1.08V`. These two corners set the narrowest common range. The
smallest measured span within a *single* corner is 0.570957773 V at
`mos_ss_125C_1.08V`. It earns **FAIL** against the unratified 0.571 V
working limit. The 0.5653 V common span is narrower because the individual
tracking windows shift across corners.

Headroom measures distance from a rail. Its largest values over all 45 points
are **319.0598 mV from VDD and 223.073866 mV from VSS**, both at
`mos_ss_125C_1.32V`. The best-case distances are 251.491128 mV from VDD at
`mos_fs_-40C_1.08V` and 141.167273 mV from VSS at `mos_ff_-40C_1.08V`.
These minima explain the historical PASS against the 251 and 141 mV minimum
working limits. Revision 4 withdrew the headroom bounds.

## CMRR at the recorded sizing

The mismatch record covers 300 valid trials at each of 45 points. Comparing
`3 * acm_lin_sigma` with the systematic common-mode gain
`10^(sys_acm0_db/20)` gives 11 points where the three-sigma term is larger.
The ratio of the Monte Carlo mean to systematic gain ranges from 0.95 to 5.67.
At the binding CMRR point, `mos_ss_-40C_1.08V`, Av0 is 44.3232 dB,
systematic gain is 4.294 V/V and the mean is 4.3058 V/V. The three-sigma
adder of 3.3155 V/V is 77.0% of the mean. The mean accounts for 56.5% of the
7.6214 V/V total.

At each point's recorded Av0, the common-mode gain budget for 50 dB CMRR is
`10^((av0_db - 50)/20)` V/V. Each row below compares its numerator with that
entire budget, then takes the maximum ratio over the grid.

| Numerator | Maximum ratio to budget | Binding point |
|---|---|---|
| Monte Carlo mean + 3 sigma | 14.651× (23.3 dB) | `mos_ss_-40C_1.08V` |
| 3 sigma alone | 6.374× | `mos_ss_-40C_1.08V` |
| Systematic common-mode gain alone | 9.172× | `mos_fs_125C_1.08V` |

Under inverse-square-root area scaling, a 6.374-fold reduction in sigma alone
would take about 41 times the input-pair area. Even if mismatch vanished,
systematic gain at `mos_fs_125C_1.08V` would still exceed the budget at
its measured Av0 by a factor of 9.172.
This grid covers one pair and mirror sizing. Predicting the effect of changing
their geometry would require new values for both Av0 and systematic gain.

## Outcome

Revision 5 records the original topology's swing range as measured evidence.
CMRR and gain remain in “Rows not submitted as bounds.” On #332, ruling r5-q96
selected a replacement topology. Amendment r5-q99 defers DR-0011 ratification
until every row has been remeasured on that design, including swing and the
45-point × 300-trial CMRR grid. Both keys then review the revised head of #303
before operator ratification. The old-topology swing evidence can land
independently. [Operator ruling and amendment](https://github.com/2AMLogic/sg13g2-opamp/issues/332)
