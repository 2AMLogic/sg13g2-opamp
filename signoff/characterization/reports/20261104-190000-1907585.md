## 3. CMRR: the mismatch-limited hypothesis is refuted

From `sim/cmrr-mismatch/records/20260921-172304-65f5fb4.csv` (N=300 x 45):

- The issue's suspected cause ("Acm +3 sigma term exceeds the structural term
  at 45/45 points") does **not** hold: `3*acm_lin_sigma > 10^(sys_acm0_db/20)`
  at only **11 of 45** points.
- At the binding point `mos_ss_-40C_1.08V` (Av0 44.3232 dB): systematic Acm
  4.30 (12.66 dB), mismatch mean 4.31, sigma 1.11, so mean+3 sigma = 4.31 + 3.32.
  The **mean is structural** and is 77 % of the +3 sigma figure.
- `acm_lin_mean / systematic` ranges 0.95 to 5.67 across the grid, so the
  structural term is not negligible anywhere.

Reduction in Acm needed to reach a 50 dB CMRR at each point's own Av0:

| If only this term were fixed | Worst-point reduction required |
|---|---|
| Whole mean+3 sigma | **14.65x (23.3 dB)** |
| Mismatch (3 sigma) alone | 6.4x |
| Structural (systematic) alone | 9.2x |

Mismatch Acm scales as `1/sqrt(W*L)` (Pelgrom), so cutting sigma 6.4x alone
needs about 41x input-pair area, and that still leaves the structural term
(9.2x too large). The structural term is set by the tail and load output
resistances, not the pair area. A resize of the pair/mirror therefore
cannot reach 50 dB; reaching it needs a topology-level change of the
common-mode path, and DR-0001 fixes the topology. Also, 41x area on the pair
moves GBW, noise, offset and Iq (the row set the issue says to re-run). This
is why no resize and no 45 x 300 grid was submitted to the batch fleet:
the records already show the result would not close the gap, and a grid that
cannot change the verdict is not a justified spend of fleet capacity.

## 4. Swing restated as an output range

Derived from the per-point absolute columns `vout_max_track_v` /
`vout_min_track_v` of `sim/output-swing/records/20260921-151759-707b34c.csv`
(45 points, tracking criterion = -6 dB incremental gain; CL = 2 pF, no
resistive load). Definition: *guaranteed output range at supply V* = from the
highest `vout_min_track_v` to the lowest `vout_max_track_v` over all
process/temperature points at that supply. The EE key owns the definition;
this is flagged for it.

| VDD | Guaranteed Vout range (over 15 process x temp points) | Span |
|---|---|---|
| 1.08 V | 0.2068 V to 0.7722 V | 0.5654 V |
| 1.20 V | 0.2150 V to 0.8894 V | 0.6744 V |
| 1.32 V | 0.2231 V to 1.0009 V | 0.7778 V |

Binding points (1.08 V): high end `mos_fs_125C_1.08V`; the 1.08 V low end
is the highest `vout_min_track_v` at that supply (0.2068 V). The per-rail headroom view (>= 251 mV from VDD at `FS/-40C`, >= 141 mV
from VSS at `FF/-40C`) is unchanged and bind at different corners, so the
range above is the single figure a buyer can read. The worst per-supply span
(0.5654 V at 1.08 V, the intersection over all corners) is *narrower* than the
ratified tracking span 0.571 V (a per-corner minimum), because the corners'
windows are shifted relative to each other. No limit changes.

## 5. Outcome

CMRR and gain cannot be defended against the comps and no resize in the fixed
topology closes the CMRR gap; both are routed to the operator (issue #332
decision block). Swing is restated. DR-0011 revision 4 records this and
stays honest that rows 1 and 2 remain submitted at their unchanged bounds
pending the operator ruling.
